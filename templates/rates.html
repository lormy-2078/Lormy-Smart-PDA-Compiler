Rate Library

Module E (Rate Engine, admin interface). Every charge incurred on a typical Import/Discharge Bulk Cargo call at Takoradi (Free Out / FIOS / FIOST terms) — grouped exactly as they appear on the two PDAs: Principal (Ship/Vessel Related Expenses + Others General) and Receiver (Cargo Related Expenses + Others General). Rates are cross-checked against the official GPHA/GMA/GSA tariffs — see each row's Source. Where a charge varies by cargo (e.g. Clinker vs. other Bulk Cargo), each cargo's rate is its own row, right next to the others, so you can see the full spread for that charge at a glance. Rows marked PLACEHOLDER aren't GPHA/GMA/GSA charges, need a driver not yet collected, or are deliberately unpriced (e.g. an export-only cargo under an import charge) — edit the amount and click Save once you have a real figure.

Filters to exactly the charges a real call for that cargo would price — every GT band and every Principal/Receiver liner-terms variant of a charge still shows (those aren't cargo-specific), only the cargo dimension narrows down. GT-band/vessel-size and liner-terms rows are unaffected since they depend on the vessel and terms, not the cargo.
